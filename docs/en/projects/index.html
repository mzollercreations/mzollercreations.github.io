01Starting point

The old site did its job – but looked like many others. Fonts and icons came from third-party servers, and a single photo weighed more than 2 MB.

02Idea

The site should feel like the treatment: slow, warm, quiet. The entire colour palette comes from the photo of the treatment room – evening light, wood, linen.

03Execution

An arch that opens into the whole room as you scroll. Treatments that glide past sideways. A breathing pause where the page goes dark. And prices you understand at a glance.
