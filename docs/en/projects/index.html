02Machine building · client confidential

Project Stack

A machine builder had developed a pneumatic pallet stacker. What was missing was the complete control engineering: pneumatic diagram, wiring diagram, control cabinet and PLC program. We built it with AI – and had everything checked automatically before a human signed it off.

✦One source of truth

Nothing gets copied by hand.

Everything comes from a single data model: devices, inputs and outputs, terminals, cables and messages. The drawings, lists and program are generated from it. Change one sensor and every document changes with it – and the checks run again from the start.

✦Digital twin

Tested before anything moves.

We didn't just write the PLC program – we ran it: against a model of the machine, one cycle every 10 milliseconds. Every movement that would be mechanically impossible or dangerous counts as a failure – including an emergency stop in every single step.

✦Automated review

Checking until there's nothing left to find.

For the second round we wrote checking programs that read every drawing by machine: overlaps, missing junction dots, terminals, cable cores, addresses. In the end there were zeros everywhere.

  • 40→0

    Lines running through labels

  • 10→0

    Cross-references pointing to the wrong column

  • 84=84

    Terminals – identical in drawing and list

  • Drawing=PLC

    Every I/O address matches between wiring diagram and program

✦Honest

What the AI did not decide.

An AI that pretends to know everything is dangerous. So every assumption is marked visibly in the drawings – and goes back to the people who are responsible for it. Seven open points, clearly named:

  1. 01Confirm the controller type
  2. 02Function of two switch-on valves
  3. 03Valve types and connector pinout
  4. 04Cylinder switches – not in the 3D model
  5. 05Process data of the sensors
  6. 06Operator panel and second emergency stop
  7. 07Risk assessment and PL verification
