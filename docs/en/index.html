✦Why sapphire

Hard on ourselves. Clear in the cut.

After diamond, sapphire is one of the hardest materials in nature. What makes it sparkle is the cut: facet by facet, every angle true to a tenth of a degree. That's how we build marketing – precise, uncompromising and cut so that nobody can overlook it.
