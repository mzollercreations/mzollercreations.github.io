03Pavilion · AI efficiency

AI that takes work off your team.

  1. 01

    Technical documents

    Wiring diagrams, parts lists and programs – generated from data you already have: CAD, descriptions, Excel.

  2. 02

    Quotes & correspondence

    An enquiry becomes a draft quote with your prices and your wording. Your team checks it and sends it.

  3. 03

    Knowledge on demand

    An assistant that knows your manuals, standards and past projects – and backs every answer with a source.

  4. 04

    Reports & data

    Figures from ERP, Excel and machines are merged, checked and turned into reports people actually read.

  5. 05

    Translation & localisation

    One source, every language – with your glossary, so technical terms are right and product names stay as they are.

  6. 06

    Checking & testing

    Test programs in simulation, check drawings against rules, reconcile lists – before a mistake gets expensive.
