04How we work

Four rules we always keep.

i

People decide.

AI delivers drafts, experts sign off. Whatever is uncertain gets marked – not hidden.

ii

Checkable, not a black box.

Every result is checked automatically: against rules, standards, simulations. You see what was checked – and what wasn't.

iii

One source of truth.

Data is maintained once. Documents, lists and code are generated from it – change something, and everything changes with it.

iv

Your data stays yours.

Before we build anything, we agree together which data each system may see. What's confidential stays confidential.

06Questions

What you might be wondering.

Will AI replace our people?

No – and that isn't the goal either. We take routine off your team so there's more time for customers, development and decisions. Responsibility and sign-off stay with you.

What happens to our data?

Before we build anything, we agree together which data may be processed and where. We treat confidential projects confidentially – that's exactly why our case study is anonymised.

Isn't this just ChatGPT?

A chat window is a start. We build tools that fit your processes: with your templates, standards and data – and with built-in checks so the results hold up.

Do we need our own AI experts?

No. We build the tools so your team can use them every day, and we show how they work.

Is it worth it for small companies too?

Especially there. Where a few people carry many tasks, every hour saved makes a difference. The calculator above gives you a first impression.

How do we start?

With a conversation about your processes. Afterwards both sides know where AI really helps – and where it doesn't.
